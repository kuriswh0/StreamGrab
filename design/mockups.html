A · Sidebar studio

Closest to the app today, just reorganised. A left sidebar replaces the top tabs, the page fills the whole window instead of stopping at 1500 px, and the queue becomes a fixed right-hand column.
  • The video gets a big preview with its stats. Options sit in three cards (Video, Chat, Bake-in), so the screen reads left to right instead of as one long form.
  • Trimming is a timeline with draggable handles, drawn over a chat-activity graph so you can see the busy moments. You can still type exact times.
  • The space below shows other videos from the same channel, one click to load them.
  • The Download button stays pinned at the bottom with a summary ("~6.1 GB · chat in 3 formats"), so you never have to scroll to it.

B · One box

A single smart input replaces the tabs. Paste anything and StreamGrab works out what it is: a VOD or clip opens Download, a channel opens Browse, a live channel offers Record, and dropped audio files open Convert. The mode chips under the box let you switch by hand.
  • A large 16:9 preview with a trim timeline under it. Chat activity is drawn on the timeline, so the busy moments are easy to find.
  • Options go in one column on the right, with bake-in styles shown as small layout thumbnails instead of a dropdown.
  • The queue is a dock along the bottom that expands when you click it, so the work area gets the full width.

C · Library

Your downloads become the centre of the app. Adding something new happens in a compact column on the left, and the middle shows everything you've downloaded, recorded or converted as a thumbnail library. Items still in progress show their progress over the thumbnail.
  • Filters (Videos, Live recordings, Clips, Audio) and search, so older downloads are easy to find. The library is built from the download folder, so it survives restarts, unlike today's queue.
  • Selecting an item opens an inspector with all its files (MP4, chat JSON/TXT, HTML replay) and the follow-up actions: re-render chat, extract audio, open the replay, show in Finder.
  • This is the biggest change. It needs a small index of finished downloads on the server side.
